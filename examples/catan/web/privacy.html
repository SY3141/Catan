HexFish Privacy Policy

Last updated June 25, 2026

This policy explains what HexFish collects, how the site uses that information, and how advertising partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies.

Information HexFish Handles

HexFish is an online game. Depending on how you use it, the site may handle:

  • Game state, moves, replay logs, room identifiers, and multiplayer connection status.
  • Profile details shown by your sign-in provider, such as your name or display name.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, request times, WebSocket events, and server logs.
  • Local settings stored in your browser, such as session identifiers, room preferences, or UI preferences.

How Information Is Used

HexFish uses this information to run the game, keep multiplayer rooms working, show replay logs, maintain account sessions, troubleshoot bugs, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

Shared replay links may be visible to anyone who has the link. Avoid putting private information into game names, player names, or other visible game content.

Cookies, Local Storage, and Advertising

HexFish may use cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies for sign-in, sessions, preferences, multiplayer behavior, analytics-like diagnostics, and advertising.

HexFish may display ads served by Google AdSense or other advertising vendors. Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to select ads based on visits to HexFish and other websites.

You can manage personalized ads from Google through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about choices for some third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info.

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use Google services at Google's partner sites policy.

Third-Party Services

HexFish may rely on third-party services to provide authentication, hosting, infrastructure, and advertising. These services may process data under their own privacy policies.

Authentication is provided through Clerk. You can review Clerk's privacy policy at clerk.com/privacy.

Your Choices

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. You can also use browser privacy controls, ad personalization controls, or third-party opt-out tools to limit some advertising behavior.

If you sign in, your account controls are managed by the authentication provider. Signing out clears your active HexFish account session in this browser.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as HexFish changes. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the HexFish site operator through the account, support, or project channel where you found the site.
